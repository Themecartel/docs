Here are answers to the most common questions about Perkstar. If you don't find what you're looking for, reach out to the Perkstar support team from the dashboard.

## Getting started

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do customers need to download an app?">
    No. Customers save their loyalty card directly to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet from their phone browser — no app download required. They simply scan your QR code or open your card link, complete the sign-up form, and tap to save the pass to their wallet.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How quickly can I set up Perkstar?">
    Most businesses have their first loyalty card live within 10 minutes. Follow the quickstart guide to create your card, customise the design, and generate your QR code — then you're ready to start signing up customers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free trial?">
    Yes. Every Perkstar plan includes a 7-day free trial with no credit card required. You get full access to all features included in your chosen plan during the trial period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Loyalty cards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which loyalty card type is right for my business?">
    It depends on how you want to reward customers:

    - **Stamp cards** work well for visit-based businesses such as cafés, salons, and barbershops, where customers earn a reward after a set number of visits.
    - **Points cards** suit spend-based businesses such as restaurants and retailers, where customers earn points proportional to how much they spend.
    - **Membership cards** are ideal for subscription businesses or those offering VIP access, where the card itself represents an ongoing membership status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have multiple loyalty cards?">
    Yes. The number of loyalty cards you can run depends on your plan. Growth plans support up to 3 active cards, and Pro plans support unlimited cards — useful if you want to run separate programmes for different locations, product lines, or customer tiers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my card design after publishing?">
    Yes. You can update your card design at any time. Any changes — including colours, logos, and reward thresholds — push automatically to all customers' saved passes in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet. Customers don't need to do anything to receive the update.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Customers

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do customers sign up?">
    Customers sign up by scanning your QR code or opening your unique card link on their phone. They complete a short sign-up form and then tap to save the loyalty card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. The whole process takes under a minute.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I import existing customers?">
    If you have an existing customer list you'd like to import, contact Perkstar support to discuss bulk customer import options for your account.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is customer data GDPR compliant?">
    Yes. Perkstar is GDPR compliant. For full details on how customer data is collected, stored, and processed, refer to the privacy policy and data processing agreement available at [perkstar.co.uk](https://perkstar.co.uk).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Scanner and rewards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need special hardware to scan cards?">
    No. The Perkstar Scanner app works on any iPhone, iPad, or Android device. There is no need for dedicated hardware or specialist equipment — your existing phones or tablets are all you need.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can multiple staff use the scanner at once?">
    Yes. Multiple staff members can log in to the Scanner app simultaneously and issue stamps or points independently. This is useful for busy periods when more than one team member is serving customers at the same time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I issue the wrong number of stamps?">
    Stamps and points can be adjusted directly from the customer's profile in the dashboard. Navigate to **Customers**, find the customer, and use the profile options to correct the balance. The customer's wallet pass updates automatically to reflect the change.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I cancel anytime?">
    Yes. You can cancel your subscription at any time from **Settings** → **Billing**. After cancelling, you retain full access to Perkstar until the end of your current billing period. You will not be charged again after that date.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens when I exceed my customer limit?">
    If your programme grows beyond your plan's customer limit, you'll be prompted to upgrade to the next plan to continue accepting new sign-ups. Your existing customers are not affected — they retain their cards and balances regardless.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
