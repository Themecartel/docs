## Annual billing

Save approximately two months' cost by switching to annual billing. To change your billing frequency, go to **Settings** → **Billing** and select the annual option for your current plan. The saving is applied immediately and your subscription renews once per year rather than monthly.

## Changing your plan

You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from **Settings** → **Billing**.

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You gain access to the additional features and customer capacity of the new plan straight away, and your billing is prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.
- **Downgrades** apply at the start of your next billing cycle. You retain access to your current plan's features until then.

## Free trial

Every new Perkstar account starts with a 7-day free trial on your chosen plan — no credit card required to begin. During the trial you have full access to all features included in your selected plan. At the end of the 7 days, choose a paid plan to continue. If you don't select a plan, your account is paused and your data is retained so you can reactivate at any time.
