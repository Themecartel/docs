Perkstar's analytics give you a clear picture of how your loyalty programme is performing at every stage — from the moment a customer signs up through to reward redemption and return visits. Use these insights to make informed decisions about your campaigns, reward structures, and customer engagement strategy.

## Accessing analytics

Navigate to **Analytics** in the left sidebar of the dashboard. Use the date range picker at the top of the page to filter all metrics by a specific time period. You can view data by the last 7 days, 30 days, 90 days, or set a custom date range to match your reporting needs.

## Key metrics

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Customers" icon="users">
    The total number of customers enrolled in your loyalty programme across all active cards.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Customers" icon="user-check">
    Customers who have visited or earned a stamp or points in the last 30, 60, or 90 days. Use this figure to gauge ongoing engagement.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stamps & Points Issued" icon="star">
    The total volume of stamps issued or points earned across your programme in the selected period. Tracks the overall level of rewards activity.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Rewards Redeemed" icon="gift">
    The number of rewards that customers have claimed. Compare this against stamps and points issued to understand your redemption rate.
  </Card>
  <Card title="New Sign-Ups" icon="user-plus">
    The number of new customers who joined your programme in the selected period, shown as a trend over time so you can see growth patterns.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Campaign Performance" icon="envelope">
    Notification delivery rates and open rates for push campaigns and automated messages, so you can see which communications drive the most engagement.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Customer retention

The active vs inactive customer breakdown shows you exactly how many of your enrolled customers are still engaging with your programme. Use this view to identify at-risk customers — those who haven't visited within your chosen window — and take action before they churn.

Once you've identified inactive segments, move to [Engage → Automated Campaigns](/engage/automated-campaigns) to set up a win-back campaign. You can target customers who haven't earned a stamp or points in a set number of days and send them an automated push notification with an incentive to return.

## Exporting data

You can export your customer data directly from the **Customers** section of the dashboard. Use the export option to download a CSV of your customer list, including names, contact details, enrolment dates, and current balances, for use in external reporting or marketing tools.

<Tip>
  Check your analytics at least once a week to spot emerging trends in sign-up volume and customer activity. Noticing a dip in active customers early gives you time to adjust your campaign timing or reward structure before engagement falls further.
</Tip>
