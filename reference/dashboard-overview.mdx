The Perkstar dashboard is your control centre for everything in your loyalty programme. From a single interface at [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), you can design loyalty cards, manage customers, run campaigns, and monitor performance — all in real time.

## Navigating the dashboard

Use the left sidebar to move between sections of the dashboard. The sidebar is visible on every page, so you can switch between Cards, Customers, Engage, Analytics, and Settings at any time. The currently active section is highlighted. On smaller screens, access the sidebar by tapping the menu icon in the top-left corner.

## Quick actions

**Create a loyalty card** — navigate to **Cards** in the sidebar, then click **Create card**. Choose a card type, customise the design and reward rules, and publish when you're ready.

**View recent sign-ups** — open the **Customers** section to see your full customer list sorted by join date. Use the date filter to focus on new sign-ups from a specific period.

**Send a push notification** — go to **Engage**, select **Push notifications**, and click **New notification**. Write your message, choose your audience, and send immediately or schedule for later.

<Note>
  All changes you make to a published loyalty card — including design updates, reward thresholds, and expiry rules — take effect immediately and push automatically to customers' Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes. Customers do not need to take any action to receive the update.
</Note>
