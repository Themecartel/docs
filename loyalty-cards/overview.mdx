Loyalty cards are the core of Perkstar. Each card you create lives directly in your customers' Apple Wallet or Google Wallet — there's no app to download and no account to create. Customers receive their card via a QR code, link, email, or SMS, tap to save it, and carry it with them on every visit.

## Choosing a card type

Perkstar offers several card formats to match different business models. Use the guidance below to pick the right starting point:

- **Stamp cards** work best for visit-based rewards — coffee shops, salons, barbers, and anywhere you want to reward customers for simply showing up.
- **Points cards** suit spend-based programmes — retailers, restaurants, and services where the value of each transaction varies and you want to reward higher spenders more.
- **Membership cards** are ideal for subscriptions, clubs, and gyms where customers pay for ongoing access to a fixed set of perks or discounts.
- **Specialist card types** (cashback, gift, coupon, multipass, tiered discount) cover more specific use cases and promotional needs.

## Creating a card

Every card type follows the same creation flow inside the Perkstar dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Cards section">
    Log in to the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk) and navigate to **Cards** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new card">
    Click **New Card** in the top-right corner of the Cards page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a card type">
    Select the card format that fits your programme — stamp, points, membership, or one of the specialist types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure rules and rewards">
    Set your earn rules, reward thresholds, and any expiry or renewal settings specific to your chosen card type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise the design">
    Add your logo, set your brand colours, name the card, and add any custom fields or wallet messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Click **Publish** to make the card live, then share it via QR code, link, email, or SMS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Card design

Every Perkstar card is fully branded to match your business. When customising your card design, you can configure:

- **Logo** — upload your business logo to display on the card face
- **Background colour** — choose a brand colour for the card background
- **Card name** — the name customers see in their wallet (e.g. "Perkstar Coffee Rewards")
- **Custom fields** — add extra information such as a website, phone number, or member ID
- **Wallet messages** — set a short message that appears on the back of the card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet

<Tip>
  Complete your card design before sharing the card link. Once customers save the card to their wallet, any subsequent design changes are pushed to them automatically — but making a strong first impression helps drive more saves.
</Tip>
