Beyond stamps, points, and memberships, Perkstar supports several specialist card types built for specific use cases and promotional goals. Whether you want to give customers cashback on their spend, sell prepaid gift cards, run a coupon campaign, bundle multiple benefits into one pass, or reward high spenders with escalating discounts — there's a card type designed for it.

## Specialist card types

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cashback Card">
    A cashback card automatically credits customers with a percentage of their spend back as credit on their card. When their cashback balance reaches a usable amount, they can spend it against a future purchase.

    **Best for:** Retailers, cafés, and service businesses that want to incentivise higher basket values and repeat spending without fixed reward tiers.

    **Setup:** Define the cashback rate as a percentage of spend (for example, 5% back on every pound). Perkstar calculates and adds the cashback credit automatically each time you record a transaction against the card.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Gift Card">
    A gift card holds a prepaid balance that the recipient can spend at your business. You issue the card with a set value, share a link or QR code with the buyer or recipient, and the balance decrements each time they use it.

    **Best for:** Gifting occasions, promotional giveaways, store credit refunds, and new customer acquisition campaigns.

    **Setup:** Set a fixed balance for the card when you create it. Share the card link directly with the recipient — they save it to their wallet and present it at the till like any other pass.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Coupon Card">
    A coupon card delivers a discount code or offer that customers save to their wallet and present in store. You can configure coupons as single-use (the card becomes void after one redemption) or multi-use (the same card can be used up to a set number of times).

    **Best for:** New customer offers, seasonal promotions, referral rewards, and partnership campaigns where you want a shareable, trackable discount.

    **Setup:** Define the discount (e.g. "20% off your first order"), set whether it's single-use or multi-use, and publish. Perkstar tracks each redemption so you can see exactly how many times a coupon has been claimed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Multipass">
    A multipass combines multiple benefits, passes, or entitlements into a single card. Customers save one card and get access to everything included in the bundle — no need to juggle separate passes.

    **Best for:** Experience bundles, event series, service packages, and businesses that want to offer several related perks under one product.

    **Setup:** Create the multipass and define each of the individual benefits or passes it contains. Customers see all included items listed on their card and you manage each entitlement from the dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Tiered Discount Card">
    A tiered discount card unlocks progressively better discounts as a customer's cumulative spend increases. The more they spend over time, the higher the discount tier they unlock — making it a powerful incentive for your highest-value customers.

    **Best for:** Repeat high-spenders, wholesale or trade customers, and businesses that want to visibly reward loyalty without running a points programme.

    **Setup:** Define each spend tier and the discount it unlocks — for example, 5% off after £100 spent, 10% off after £250, and 15% off after £500. The card in the customer's wallet updates automatically as they move up the tiers.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Choosing the right card type

Not sure which specialist card to use? Here's a quick comparison:

| Card type | Reward mechanism | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Cashback** | % of spend returned as credit | Repeat purchasers, higher basket values |
| **Gift card** | Prepaid balance to spend | Gifting, store credit, promotions |
| **Coupon** | Fixed discount on redemption | New customers, seasonal campaigns |
| **Multipass** | Bundle of benefits in one card | Service packages, event series |
| **Tiered discount** | Escalating discount by spend level | High-value repeat customers |

If you're looking for a straightforward visit or spend-based programme, stamp and points cards are usually the better starting point.
