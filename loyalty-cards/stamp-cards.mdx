A stamp card rewards customers every time they visit or make a purchase. Once they collect enough stamps, they unlock a reward of your choice. Stamp cards are a natural fit for coffee shops, salons, barbers, and any business where the goal is to bring customers back repeatedly — they're simple to understand, easy to use, and need no app download.

## How stamp cards work

Each time a customer visits or completes a transaction, you issue them one stamp through the Perkstar dashboard or the Perkstar Scanner app. The card in their Apple Wallet or Google Wallet updates instantly to show their progress.

When the customer reaches the stamp target you set, their reward is unlocked automatically and displayed on their card. You can then mark the reward as redeemed when they claim it in store, resetting the card ready for the next cycle.

## Setting up a stamp card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Cards → New Card">
    Log in to the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Cards** in the sidebar, and click **New Card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Stamp Card">
    Choose **Stamp Card** from the list of available card types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the number of stamps required">
    Enter how many stamps a customer must collect to earn a reward — for example, 10 stamps for a free coffee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the reward">
    Give the reward a name and description that customers will see on their card, such as "Free coffee" or "50% off your next visit".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise your card design">
    Upload your logo, choose a background colour, and set the card name. These appear on the card face in your customers' wallets.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Click **Publish** to make your stamp card live. Share it with customers via QR code, direct link, email, or SMS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Customisation options

Stamp cards give you control over how the programme looks and behaves:

- **Stamps per reward** — set any number as your target (e.g. 6, 8, 10, or 12 stamps)
- **Reward name and description** — write exactly what the customer receives so there's no ambiguity at the till
- **Partial stamp display** — customers can see how many stamps they've collected at a glance directly in their wallet
- **Card colours** — match the card background and text to your brand palette

## Issuing stamps

You can issue stamps to customers in two ways:

- **Perkstar Scanner** — use the Scanner app on your phone or tablet to scan a customer's card and issue a stamp instantly. See [Using the Scanner](/scanner/using-the-scanner) for setup instructions.
- **Dashboard** — search for a customer in the Perkstar dashboard and issue stamps manually from their profile.

<Note>
  You can issue stamps both in store using the Scanner and remotely from the dashboard. Remote issuance is useful for online orders or deliveries where the customer isn't physically present.
</Note>
