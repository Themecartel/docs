A points card lets customers build up a balance of points over time and redeem them for rewards once they hit a threshold. Because points scale with spend, customers who spend more earn more — making points cards a great fit for retailers, restaurants, and any service where transaction values vary. Customers carry their card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and see their live points balance update after every visit.

## How points cards work

Customers earn points either per pound spent or per visit, depending on the earn rules you set. Their points total is displayed on the card in their wallet and updates automatically each time you issue points.

When a customer's balance reaches a redemption threshold you've defined, they become eligible for a reward. You choose what the reward is and whether customers can redeem at multiple tiers (for example, 100 points for a discount and 250 points for a free product).

## Setting up a points card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Cards → New Card">
    Log in to the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Cards** in the sidebar, and click **New Card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Points Card">
    Choose **Points Card** from the list of available card types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the earn rate">
    Define how many points customers earn — for example, 1 point per £1 spent, or a fixed number of points per visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set redemption thresholds">
    Add one or more point thresholds at which customers become eligible to redeem a reward. You can add multiple tiers to offer different rewards at different levels.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the reward at each threshold">
    Name the reward and describe exactly what the customer receives when they reach each tier — for example, "£5 off" at 100 points or "Free item of your choice" at 500 points.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise your design and publish">
    Upload your logo, set your brand colours, name the card, then click **Publish**. Share the card with customers via QR code, link, email, or SMS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Points earn rules

You have flexibility over how customers accumulate points:

- **Per-spend earn rate** — award a set number of points for every pound (or fraction of a pound) the customer spends
- **Per-visit earn rate** — award a fixed number of points each time a customer visits, regardless of transaction value
- **Manual point issuance** — add points to a customer's card directly from the dashboard at any time, useful for compensating a customer or running a one-off promotion

## Rewards and redemption

When a customer reaches a redemption threshold, they can present their card for a reward. You mark the redemption in the dashboard or via the Perkstar Scanner, which deducts the redeemed points from their balance. You can configure whether redeemed points reset the balance to zero or simply reduce it by the threshold amount, allowing customers to continue accumulating towards the next tier.

Reward expiry is optional — you can set a window in which a customer must redeem their unlocked reward before it lapses.

<Tip>
  Use milestone automations to send customers a push notification or wallet message when they're close to a reward threshold. Reminding customers they're only 10 points away from a reward is one of the most effective ways to drive a return visit.
</Tip>
