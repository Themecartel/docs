A membership card gives customers a persistent digital card that proves their membership status and surfaces the perks they're entitled to. Unlike stamp or points cards, membership cards aren't about accumulation — they're about access. They suit gyms, clubs, subscription boxes, professional associations, and any business that sells ongoing membership or wants to reward a VIP tier of customers with fixed benefits.

## How membership cards work

When a customer saves a membership card to their Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, it displays their membership name, the perks or discounts they're entitled to, and their current membership status. You control that status from the Perkstar dashboard — when a membership is active, the card shows as valid; when it lapses or you revoke it, the card updates automatically in the customer's wallet.

Because the card lives in the wallet, members always have it with them and can present it instantly without searching for an email or logging into an account.

## Setting up a membership card

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Cards → New Card">
    Log in to the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Cards** in the sidebar, and click **New Card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Membership Card">
    Choose **Membership Card** from the list of available card types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the membership name and benefits">
    Give the membership a clear name (e.g. "Gold Member", "Annual Pass") and write a benefits description that customers will see on the back of their card — for example, "10% off every visit" or "Unlimited classes included".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set expiry or renewal period">
    If the membership runs for a fixed term, set an expiry date or renewal interval (e.g. 12 months from issue). Leave this blank for open-ended memberships you manage manually.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customise your card design">
    Upload your logo, choose a background colour, and set the card name. A well-designed membership card reinforces the premium feel of belonging.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and share">
    Click **Publish** to make the membership card live. Share it with members via QR code, direct link, email, or SMS.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Displaying membership status

Perkstar automatically reflects the current status of each membership card in the customer's wallet:

- **Active** — the membership is valid and the card displays normally with all perk information visible
- **Expired** — if the membership has passed its expiry date, the card updates to show an expired state, prompting the member to renew

Both states are pushed to the customer's wallet in real time — you don't need to ask customers to refresh or re-save anything.

## Use cases

Membership cards work across a wide range of business types:

- **Gym and fitness** — issue a digital access card that doubles as proof of membership at the door
- **VIP and loyalty tiers** — reward your top customers with a branded VIP card that shows their exclusive discount
- **Subscriber perks** — give subscribers to a box, service, or newsletter a card that lists what they're entitled to and when it renews

<Note>
  You manage each member's status manually from the Perkstar dashboard. If you run a paid membership, make sure to update or expire a member's card when their payment lapses to keep your records accurate and prevent unauthorised use of member perks.
</Note>
