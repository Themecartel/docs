When a customer scans your QR code or opens your card link, they complete a short sign-up form before saving the card to their wallet. This is your opportunity to collect the information that matters most to your business — from contact details to personal preferences.

## Default fields

Every sign-up form collects a customer's **name** and **email address** by default. These fields are always present and cannot be removed, ensuring you always have a reliable way to identify and contact your customers.

## Adding custom fields

You can extend the sign-up form with any additional fields your programme requires. Follow these steps to add one:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your card in the dashboard">
    Go to [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Cards**, and select the card you want to edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Edit">
    Click **Edit** to open the card editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Sign-up Form section">
    Scroll to, or select, the **Sign-up Form** section within the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new field">
    Click **Add Field** to create a new form field.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the field type">
    Select the type that best suits the information you want to collect: **Text**, **Date**, **Dropdown**, or **Checkbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Label the field">
    Enter a clear, customer-facing label for the field — for example, *Date of Birth* or *Phone Number*.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as required or optional">
    Choose whether customers must fill in this field before submitting the form, or whether it is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save** to apply the updated form to your card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Adding a **Date of Birth** field unlocks birthday reward automations, letting you automatically send a reward to customers on their birthday. Learn more in [Automated Campaigns](/engage/automated-campaigns).
</Note>

## Customer data privacy

All customer data collected through your sign-up form is stored securely and in line with GDPR requirements. Customers can unsubscribe from communications at any time. Perkstar does not share your customer data with third parties.

## Viewing customer records

Every customer who signs up is stored in your dashboard. To view your customer list:

1. Go to [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk) and click **Customers** in the navigation.
2. Select any customer to open their profile.
3. From their profile, you can view their individual card details, full transaction history, and current points or stamps balance.
