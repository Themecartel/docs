Once your loyalty card is published, you can start sharing it with customers straight away. Perkstar gives you several ways to get your card in front of people — whether they're standing at your counter or browsing online.

## Sharing methods

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="QR Code">
    Download your card's QR code from the dashboard and display it wherever your customers will see it — printed on your counter, shown on a screen, or added to receipts and packaging.

    When a customer scans the QR code with their phone camera, they're taken directly to your sign-up form. Once they complete it, they tap **Add to Wallet** to save the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

    <Tip>
      Print your QR code and place it prominently at your point of sale. A small countertop stand or a sticker near the till makes it easy for customers to scan without asking staff.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Link">
    Every loyalty card has a unique shareable URL. Copy it from the dashboard and paste it anywhere your customers already are — email newsletters, social media posts, your website, or a WhatsApp message.

    Customers who tap the link are taken to the same sign-up experience as scanning the QR code.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Email">
    Send your card link directly to customers from within the dashboard. You can send to an individual customer or to a list, making it easy to invite existing customers to join your loyalty programme.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="SMS">
    Send your card link via text message from the dashboard. SMS is one of the highest-engagement channels — a short message with your card link gets customers signed up in seconds.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  Customers do **not** need to download any app. They simply tap **Add to Wallet** after signing up, and the loyalty card is saved directly to their Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
</Note>

## Downloading your QR code

To download your card's QR code:

1. Go to [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk) and navigate to **Cards**.
2. Select the card you want to share.
3. Click the **Share** tab.
4. Click **Download QR** to save the image to your device.

You can then print the QR code or use it in any digital materials.

## Tracking sign-ups

Keep an eye on how your card is performing from the dashboard analytics. Sign-up numbers update in real time, so you can see exactly how many customers have joined your programme and when they signed up.
