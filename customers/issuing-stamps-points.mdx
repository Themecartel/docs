Perkstar gives you multiple ways to add stamps and points to your customers' loyalty cards — whether they're standing in front of you, placing an order online, or somewhere in between. Choose the method that fits your workflow, or combine them for a seamless omnichannel experience.

## In-store with the Scanner app

The fastest way to issue stamps and points at the point of sale is with the **Perkstar Scanner app**, available on any phone or tablet.

When a customer is ready to collect their stamp or points, they open their loyalty card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet and present the barcode on screen. A staff member scans it with the Scanner app, and the stamp or points are added instantly.

No extra hardware is needed — any device with a camera works.

<Tip>
  Use the Perkstar Scanner app for the quickest in-person experience. It takes just a few seconds per customer and works on any iOS or Android device your team already has.
</Tip>

Learn how to set up and use the Scanner app in [Using the Scanner](/scanner/using-the-scanner).

## From the dashboard

You can also issue stamps or points manually from the Perkstar dashboard. This is useful for correcting a missed transaction or rewarding a customer who contacted you after their visit.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Customers">
    Navigate to [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk) and click **Customers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the customer">
    Use the search bar to find the customer by name or email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open their profile">
    Click on the customer's name to open their profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a stamp or points">
    Click **Add stamp** or **Add points**, depending on your card type, and enter the amount to issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the transaction">
    Review the details and click **Confirm**. The customer's balance updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Issuing points remotely

For online orders or situations where a customer isn't physically present, you can send a points link or notification directly to the customer. They tap the link to claim their points, which are added to their card automatically. This makes it straightforward to reward customers for purchases made through your website or over the phone.

## Bulk issuance

Need to reward a group of customers at once? You can issue stamps or points to a customer segment directly from the dashboard — useful for running a promotion, rewarding loyal customers, or reactivating lapsed ones.

## Correcting mistakes

If stamps or points are issued in error, you can adjust or reverse them from the customer's profile in the dashboard. Navigate to **Customers**, open the relevant customer's profile, and edit the transaction to correct the balance.

<Note>
  Perkstar supports integrations that can automatically issue points when a customer makes a purchase — no manual action required. See [Integrations](/reference/integrations) for the full list of supported platforms.
</Note>
