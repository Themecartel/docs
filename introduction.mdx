Perkstar is a digital loyalty platform built for businesses that want to reward repeat customers without the complexity of a traditional loyalty programme. Whether you run a café, salon, gym, restaurant, retail store, service brand, or franchise, Perkstar gives you the tools to create branded digital loyalty cards, share them instantly with customers, and track every interaction — all from a single dashboard.

## How Perkstar works

You create a loyalty card in the Perkstar dashboard, choosing the card type and reward rules that suit your business. Once your card is ready, you share it with customers using a QR code, a direct link, email, or SMS. Customers save the card to their Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in a single tap. From that point on, your staff issue stamps or points using the Perkstar Scanner app on any phone or tablet. As customers earn rewards, Perkstar automatically tracks their progress and can trigger campaigns — such as birthday perks or win-back offers — on your behalf.

## Card types

Perkstar supports a range of card formats so you can match the loyalty mechanic to your business model.

- **Stamp card** — Award a stamp for each visit or purchase; customers redeem a reward once they collect enough stamps.
- **Points card** — Let customers accumulate points based on spend or activity and redeem them against future purchases.
- **Membership card** — Give members a digital card that identifies their membership status and any associated benefits.
- **Cashback card** — Return a percentage of spend as credit that customers can use on a future visit.
- **Gift card** — Issue digital gift cards with a set value that customers can spend in your business.
- **Coupon card** — Distribute single-use or multi-use digital coupons redeemable at the point of sale.
- **Multipass card** — Bundle multiple visits or sessions into a single pre-paid card (ideal for classes or treatments).
- **Tiered discount card** — Unlock higher discount levels as customers reach spending or visit milestones.

## No app required

Customers don't need to download anything to use a Perkstar loyalty card. When they receive your card link or scan your QR code, they're taken directly to a page where they can save the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet with a single tap. The card lives on their phone alongside their boarding passes and payment cards, so it's always easy to find. Updates — such as new stamps or a redeemed reward — appear automatically on the card without any action needed from the customer.
