Before you create your first loyalty card, take a few minutes to set up your Perkstar account properly. A complete business profile ensures your branding appears consistently across every card and customer-facing communication, and getting your team access early means everyone is ready to start issuing rewards from day one.

<Tip>
  Complete your business profile — including your logo, business name, and contact details — before you create any loyalty cards. Perkstar pulls your profile information into new cards automatically, so doing this first saves you time and keeps your branding consistent from the start.
</Tip>

## Creating your account

Go to [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-up](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-up) and enter your name, email address, and a password. After you submit the form, Perkstar sends a verification email to the address you provided. Open that email and click the confirmation link to activate your account. Once verified, you're taken directly to your dashboard and your 7-day free trial begins.

If you already have an account, sign in at [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-in](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-in).

## Business profile

Your business profile is the foundation of your loyalty programme. To set it up, go to **Settings** in the left-hand navigation and select **Business Profile**. From there, fill in:

- **Business name** — This appears on your loyalty cards and in customer-facing communications.
- **Logo** — Upload a high-quality version of your logo. It's displayed on every card you create.
- **Contact details** — Add your website, phone number, and address so customers can easily get in touch.

Save your changes and your profile information will be available to use whenever you create or edit a card.

## Inviting team members

You can give your staff access to the Perkstar dashboard and Scanner app so they can issue stamps, add points, and manage customer interactions without needing to share your login credentials. To invite a team member:

1. Go to **Settings** and select **Team**.
2. Click **Invite Member** and enter the team member's email address.
3. Assign them a role and send the invitation.

Your team member receives an email with a link to set up their own Perkstar login. Once they've accepted the invitation, they can sign in to the Scanner app and the dashboard using their own credentials.

## Choosing a plan

Perkstar offers three plans so you can start small and scale as your loyalty programme grows:

- **Starter** (£19/month) — 1 loyalty card, up to 250 customers
- **Growth** (£39/month) — 3 loyalty cards, up to 1,000 customers
- **Pro** (£79/month) — Unlimited loyalty cards and customers

All plans include a 7-day free trial so you can explore the platform before committing. For a full breakdown of what's included in each plan, see the [Pricing Plans](/reference/pricing-plans) page.

## Billing and subscription

Manage your subscription at any time from **Settings → Billing** in your dashboard. From the billing page you can:

- **Upgrade your plan** if you need more cards or higher customer limits
- **Downgrade your plan** if your needs change
- **Update your payment details**
- **View past invoices**

Changes to your subscription take effect immediately, and you're only ever charged for what you use.
