Wallet messages are text fields displayed directly on your loyalty card, visible to customers every time they open their pass in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. Unlike push notifications, which appear once on the lock screen, wallet messages are always present on the card — making them ideal for ongoing promotions, important information, or anything you want customers to see at the point of redemption.

## Types of wallet messages

Your Perkstar loyalty card supports several message fields, each appearing in a different position on the pass:

- **Header message** — displayed prominently at the top of the card, beneath your business name. Use this for your most important current message, such as an active promotion or seasonal offer.
- **Secondary message** — appears below the header. Use this for supporting detail, such as offer terms or a secondary call to action.
- **Back-of-card fields** — additional text fields shown on the reverse of the pass. Customers see these when they tap the information button on their card. Use these for terms and conditions, opening hours, contact details, or any information that is useful but doesn't need to be front and centre.

## Updating wallet messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your card editor">
    In the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Cards**, then select the loyalty card you want to update and click **Edit**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Wallet Messages">
    Scroll to the **Wallet Messages** section within the card editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your message fields">
    Edit the header message, secondary message, or back-of-card fields as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Click **Save**. Perkstar automatically pushes the updated text to all customers who have saved your card — no action required from them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Card updates are pushed automatically to every saved pass. Customers do not need to re-add the card or take any action — their loyalty card updates silently in the background as soon as you save your changes.
</Note>

## Use cases

Wallet messages work well for any information you want customers to see when they open their card:

- **Seasonal promotions** — *"Buy 5 coffees in December, get one free."*
- **New opening hours** — keep customers informed when your hours change over holidays or due to refurbishment.
- **Expiring reward reminders** — prompt customers to redeem before their reward expires.
- **New menu items or products** — a quick way to broadcast news to your most loyal customers.

## Character limits

Apple Wallet and Google Wallet display a limited number of characters on the front of the card before truncating text. Keep your header and secondary messages short and punchy — aim for under 60 characters for front-of-card fields so your message is always fully visible without customers needing to scroll or expand.

Back-of-card fields support longer text, making them a better home for detailed terms, contact information, or multi-line content.

<Tip>
  Use wallet messages alongside push notifications for maximum visibility. Update the wallet message with your current offer first, then send a push notification to draw customers' attention to it. Customers who tap the notification will land directly on the card and immediately see the updated message.
</Tip>
