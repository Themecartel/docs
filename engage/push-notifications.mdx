Push notifications let you reach customers directly on their phones without requiring a separate app. When you send a notification from the Perkstar dashboard, it is delivered as a wallet pass update — customers see it on their lock screen the moment it arrives, and tapping it opens their loyalty card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.

## How wallet push notifications work

When you send a push notification, every customer who has saved your loyalty card to their wallet receives a lock-screen notification. The message appears under your business name, exactly like a standard system notification. Tapping the notification opens their loyalty card directly, putting your offer or message front and centre at the moment they engage.

No third-party app is required for customers — the notifications are delivered natively through Apple Wallet and Google Wallet.

## Sending a notification

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notifications">
    In the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Engage → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new notification">
    Click **New Notification** to open the message composer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message">
    Enter your notification text. Keep it to one or two sentences — concise messages perform better and are less likely to be dismissed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your audience">
    Choose who receives the notification. You can send to all customers or target a specific segment (see [Audience segmentation](#audience-segmentation) below).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or send immediately">
    Either send the notification right away or pick a future date and time to schedule delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm send">
    Review the message and audience summary, then click **Send** (or **Schedule**) to confirm.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Audience segmentation

Rather than messaging every customer every time, target the right people with the right message. Perkstar lets you send notifications to:

- **All customers** — everyone who has saved your loyalty card
- **Customers with X or more stamps / points** — reward your most loyal visitors
- **Inactive customers** — reach customers who haven't visited recently
- **Birthday customers this month** — send a timely birthday offer to customers celebrating this month

Targeting a specific segment helps keep your messages relevant and reduces the chance of customers removing their card.

## Best practices

- **Keep messages short.** One clear sentence is more effective than a paragraph. Customers decide in a second whether to tap or dismiss.
- **Include a clear call to action.** Tell customers exactly what to do — for example, *"Show this card in-store today for a free coffee."*
- **Don't over-send.** Aim for a maximum of one to two notifications per week. Customers who feel bombarded will remove their card.
- **Send at the right time.** Early afternoon or just before typical visit times tends to drive the best engagement.

<Warning>
  Sending too many push notifications is the most common reason customers remove a loyalty card from their wallet. Stick to a maximum of one or two messages per week, and only send when you have something genuinely valuable to share.
</Warning>
