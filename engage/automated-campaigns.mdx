Automations run in the background so you never miss an opportunity to engage a customer at exactly the right moment. Once you activate an automation, Perkstar handles the timing and delivery for you — no manual sending required, even during your busiest periods.

## Setting up an automation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automations">
    In the [Perkstar dashboard](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk), navigate to **Engage → Automations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new automation">
    Click **New Automation** to open the automation builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an automation type">
    Select the type of automation you want to set up — for example, Birthday Reward, Win-Back Campaign, or Milestone Reward.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure the trigger">
    Set the conditions that trigger the automation. Depending on the type, this might be a customer's birthday, a number of days since their last visit, or a specific stamp or point count.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your message and attach a reward">
    Enter the message customers will receive. Optionally attach a reward — such as a bonus stamp, free item, or points boost — to make the automation more compelling.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the automation">
    Toggle the automation to **Active**. Perkstar will now run it automatically in the background.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Birthday rewards

Birthday automations send a reward or message to customers on their birthday — or a set number of days before, so they have time to visit.

For birthday automations to work, customers must have provided their date of birth when joining your loyalty programme. Make sure your customer sign-up form includes a birthday field. See [Customer Sign-Up Forms](/customers/customer-sign-up) for instructions on adding this field.

## Win-back campaigns

Win-back automations re-engage customers who haven't visited your business in a defined period. When you set up a win-back campaign, you choose the inactivity window — common choices are 30, 60, or 90 days. When a customer hasn't scanned their card within that window, Perkstar automatically sends them your win-back message and any attached offer.

<Tip>
  Combine your win-back automation with a bonus stamp offer — for example, *"We miss you! Visit this week and we'll double your stamps."* A tangible incentive gives lapsed customers a reason to return rather than just a reminder that they haven't visited.
</Tip>
