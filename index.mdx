## Get up and running

Follow these four steps to go from sign-up to your first rewarded customer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-up](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-up) and start your 7-day free trial. No credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design your card">
    Choose a card type, add your logo and brand colours, and configure your reward rules — all from the Cards section of your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with customers">
    Distribute your card via QR code, shareable link, email, or SMS. Customers tap once to save it directly to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start rewarding">
    Use the Perkstar Scanner app or your dashboard to issue stamps or points every time a customer visits or makes a purchase.
  </Step>
</Steps>
