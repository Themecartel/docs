---
title: "Get Started with Perkstar: Create Your First Loyalty Card"
sidebarTitle: "Quickstart"
description: "Set up your first digital loyalty card on Perkstar in under 10 minutes. Create your account, design your card, and share it with customers."
---

This guide walks you through everything you need to do to go from zero to your first rewarded customer. By the end, you'll have a branded digital loyalty card live in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, ready for customers to save and start collecting rewards.

<Note>
  All Perkstar plans include a 7-day free trial. You don't need a credit card to sign up and start exploring the platform.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-up](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-up) and enter your name, email address, and a password to create your account. Once you've signed up, check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link to activate your account. Your 7-day free trial starts immediately — no credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a card type">
    In the dashboard, navigate to **Cards** and select **New Card**. Perkstar offers several card types — choose the one that best fits your business:

    - **Stamp card** — Great for cafés, bakeries, and food businesses where customers earn a reward after a set number of visits.
    - **Points card** — Ideal for retailers or restaurants where you want to reward based on spend amount.
    - **Membership card** — Perfect for gyms, clubs, or subscription businesses that need to identify members.
    - **Cashback, gift, coupon, multipass, or tiered discount** — Choose one of these specialist formats if your business model calls for it.

    If you're not sure where to start, a stamp card is the quickest to set up and the most familiar format for customers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Design your card">
    Brand your card so it's instantly recognisable to your customers. From the card editor, you can:

    - Upload your **logo**
    - Set your **brand colours** for the card background and text
    - Enter a **card name** (for example, "Coffee Loyalty Card" or "VIP Members Card")
    - Configure your **reward rules**, such as the number of stamps needed to earn a reward or the points value per pound spent

    All changes are previewed in real time so you can see exactly how the card will look in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet before you publish it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share your card">
    Once your card is published, Perkstar generates several ways to share it with customers. Head to the **Share** tab on your card to access:

    - **QR code** — Print or display it in your store so customers can scan and save the card instantly
    - **Direct link** — Copy and paste into any message, email, or social media post
    - **Email** — Send the card directly to a customer's inbox from the dashboard
    - **SMS** — Text the card link straight to a customer's phone

    When a customer opens the link or scans the QR code, they're taken to a page where they can save the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet in a single tap — no app download needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start issuing rewards">
    Download the **Perkstar Scanner** app on any phone or tablet and sign in with your Perkstar account. To issue a stamp or add points, open the app and scan the customer's loyalty card from their Apple Wallet or Google Wallet. The card updates instantly on the customer's device. You can also issue stamps and points manually from the customer's profile in your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>
