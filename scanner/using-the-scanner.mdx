Issuing a stamp or points takes under 10 seconds with the Perkstar Scanner. Once your device is set up, the process is a single scan and a single tap — fast enough to keep queues moving even during your busiest periods.

## Setup

Complete these steps once on each device you want to use as a scanner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Perkstar Scanner app">
    Search for **Perkstar Scanner** on the App Store (iPhone or iPad) or Google Play (Android) and install the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open the app and sign in using your Perkstar dashboard credentials. You can find the sign-in page at [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-in](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk/sign-in) if you need a reminder of your email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your loyalty card">
    If your Perkstar account has more than one loyalty card, select the card you want to issue stamps or points for. You can switch between cards at any time from within the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're ready to scan">
    The app is now ready. Keep it open on your device at the counter so it's available the moment a customer presents their pass.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Issuing a stamp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask the customer to open their loyalty card">
    Ask the customer to open your loyalty card in Apple Wallet or Google Wallet on their phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the scan button">
    In the Perkstar Scanner app, tap the **Scan** button to activate the camera.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan the barcode">
    Point your device's camera at the barcode displayed on the customer's pass. The app recognises the code automatically — there is no need to press a button to capture it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the stamp">
    The app displays the customer's name and current stamp count. Tap **Confirm** to issue the stamp. The customer's loyalty card updates instantly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Issuing points

Issuing points follows the same scanning flow as stamps. After the app identifies the customer's pass, you will be prompted to enter either a **points amount** or a **transaction value** (depending on how your loyalty programme is configured). Enter the amount and tap **Confirm** — the points are added to the customer's card immediately.

## Multiple staff members

Each staff member downloads the Perkstar Scanner app on their own device and logs in using the same Perkstar account credentials. There is no limit to the number of devices that can be logged in simultaneously. Every scan made by any staff member syncs to your Perkstar dashboard in real time, giving you a complete record of all activity across your team.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion title="The barcode won't scan">
  Poor lighting is the most common cause of scanning difficulty. Make sure the customer's screen is well lit — ask them to increase their screen brightness to maximum. Avoid scanning in direct sunlight, which can wash out the barcode. If the camera still struggles, try moving slightly closer or further from the pass until the app locks on.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="The customer's card isn't updating">
  Check that your device has an active internet connection. The Perkstar Scanner app continues to work offline and stores scans locally, then syncs them automatically as soon as the connection is restored. If the card hasn't updated after a few minutes, ask the customer to close and reopen their wallet pass to trigger a refresh.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I've selected the wrong loyalty card">
  Tap the **Back** button or the card selector at the top of the scan screen to return to the card selection screen. Select the correct loyalty programme and then proceed with the scan as normal.
</Accordion>

<Tip>
  During busy periods, keep the Perkstar Scanner app open and active on a dedicated counter device — such as a tablet in a stand — so your team can scan without having to unlock phones or navigate to the app. The scan screen stays active as long as the app is in the foreground.
</Tip>
