The Perkstar Scanner app turns any phone or tablet into a loyalty scanner, so any staff member can issue stamps and points directly from a device they already have in their pocket. There is no dedicated hardware to purchase, no complicated setup, and no separate system to manage — just download the app, log in, and start scanning.

## What you need

The Perkstar Scanner app works on any modern smartphone or tablet:

- **iPhone or iPad** — download from the App Store
- **Android phone or tablet** — download from Google Play

Log in with the same account credentials you use to access the Perkstar dashboard at [dashboard.perkstar.co.uk](https://dashboard.perkstar.co.uk). No separate Scanner account is required.

## Getting started

Getting set up takes just a few minutes:

1. **Download** the Perkstar Scanner app from the App Store or Google Play.
2. **Log in** with your Perkstar account credentials.
3. **Start scanning** customer wallet passes to issue stamps and points.

For a full step-by-step walkthrough — including issuing points, handling multiple loyalty cards, and troubleshooting — see the [Using the Scanner](/scanner/using-the-scanner) guide.

<Note>
  The Perkstar Scanner app is included with all Perkstar plan subscriptions. There is no additional charge to install the app on multiple devices or for multiple staff members to use it simultaneously.
</Note>
